science Empirical Findings

The Decoupled Dual-Branch approach successfully isolates texture from structural morphological features, aiming to mimic expert dermatologist visual analysis.

"While the dual-branch architecture introduces novel attention mechanisms for feature fusion, benchmark testing revealed an unexpected performance delta."

Subsequent validation demonstrated that the monolithic EfficientNet-B4 baseline, utilizing compound scaling, generalized more robustly across diverse lesion datasets than the custom decoupled architecture.
